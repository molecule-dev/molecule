### Minor Changes

- Render the shared markdown DOM: `irc:`, `ircs:` and `xmpp:` links, real ids for non-Latin headings, no `target` for `linkTarget: '_self'`, titles escaped once, spaces in URLs encoded, and `elementAttributes`; `components` now throws, and `isSafeUrl` and `slugify` are deprecated in favour of the core functions.
