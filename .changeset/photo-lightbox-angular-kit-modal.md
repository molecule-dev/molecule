The lightbox opens as a kit modal: Escape closes only the top dialog, arrow keys move between photos, and focus returns to the opener. Requires `@molecule/app-angular` 3.13.0, `@molecule/app-ui` 1.10.0 and `@molecule/app-ui-angular` 1.7.0 or later.
